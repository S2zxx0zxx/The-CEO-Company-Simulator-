## Implementation
New src/rail/economy/energy.ts, original pure code. estimateEnergy resolves exact R12 catalog reference and supported nominal rate per mode (missing rate fails closed; no zero default); unpowered stock rejected; diesel mL, electricity Wh, steam g. Per segment distance, running/idle seconds, explicit load/consist and grade factors (1..100000 bps engineering bound), idle/aux quantity per hour supplied by caller. Traction = ceil(nominal*metres*loadBps*gradeBps/100000000000), idle+aux = ceil((idleRate*idleS+auxRate*(runningS+idleS))/3600). Electric recovery = min(requested Wh, floor(traction*maxRegenBps/10000)), only electric, no negative net or export. Round per segment explicitly, not calibrated physics; segment granularity affects ceil, future R33/R37 must choose canonical segmentation and trustworthy load/grade/time/regen inputs. Zero consumption omitted from positive energy requirements, aggregate unit-safe canonical R06 quantities. No actual train operational movement integration yet.

Purchases enforce nonnegative exact paise, positive energy amount, capacity inclusive of reserved stock, unique lot ID, declared available cash check (trusted current cash supplied at transaction boundary, not a cash ledger). Spot purchase locks explicit price per positive quantity, ceil purchase cost; contract purchases validate exact unit, total original price, original quantity and ID, cap cumulative draws, apportion by difference of cumulative floors; total full draw equals original contract price. Individual fractional paise rounding depends on canonical settlement order (documented); unit rates and lot selection fixed, no spot repricing.
