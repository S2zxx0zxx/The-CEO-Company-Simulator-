## Implementation decisions
src/rail/economy/demand.ts (new, no change to legacy engine). R06 strict decoders and units reused. E01 accepts integer base and cap, canonical minor-unit fare/reference, positive generalized/reference seconds, signed elasticities -40000..0 bps, season/quality 0..100000 bps, explicit min/max fare/time ratio bounds 100..1000000 bps bracketing one. Engineering input ceilings, not calibrated defaults. Free fare clamps to provided lower ratio. BigInt fixed-point scale 10^12, integer exponent and 32 binary fractional steps via integer square root, floor intermediate factors and output, final cap. Deterministic without platform-specific Math.pow; approximation needs exact formula comparison and numeric accuracy/calibration review R41. No ambient time/random. Passenger demand counts and freight kg selected by kind; freight-specific rates/calibration deferred, same elasticity function applies generalized charge/time.
E02 accepts explicit nonnegative integer normalized utility weights and positive outside weight; exact rational Hamilton allocation with stable ties outside then ASCII service ID. No utility coefficient/default logit inferred. Zero-weight services cannot receive allocation. Returns full partition of current available demand; outside is a forecast, not booked stock. Repeated dispatch recomputes shares on remaining available stock; no durable market quota across dispatch windows claimed. Caller must supply trusted service weights from current policy at integration.
E03 creates canonical immutable DemandBook v1 with R06 DemandBucket entries tagged passenger/freight; unique IDs and disjoint half-open intervals per directed kind/OD; buckets reconcile available+reserved+served=total and reserved/served sums to recorded claims. Strictly decode copied inputs and canonical order, reject malformed or tampered restore. Reserve command checks expected revision, exact stop indices and station IDs, dispatch within bucket interval, one claim per bucket per trip, unique trip ID, current service market quota, min(request, quota, available, remaining capacity on all traversed segments); separate seats and kg, adjacent segment reuse, ordered tie allocation by board then alight then bucket ID, records unmet units. Entire batch is pure candidate; invalid later claim leaves input unchanged. closeDemand transitions each whole trip once to served or released; terminal identity retained, no duplicate delivery or re-reservation; no actual revenue. Revision stale/overflow rejected. Limits: <=100000 buckets, <=10000 retained trips, <=32 stops, <=128 claims per trip, <=10000 service weights. Limits fail closed, not measured capacity; archival/partial stop event lifecycle R33/R39. Demand snapshot validation checks aggregate quantities/caps, not trusted prior market calculation authenticity. R34/R39 must commit this pure transition using actual authoritative CAS in single transaction together with cash/energy/track/train, enforce trusted current scenario/weights, command replay and partial settlement. No multi-tab/database concurrency or production dispatch wired at R21.
