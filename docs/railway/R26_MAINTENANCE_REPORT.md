## Implementation and decisions
Extend existing src/rail/economy/operations.ts replay, not a parallel condition or odometer store. Backward compatible R25 schemaVersion 1 historical event shapes and train projection preserved. Five new strict events: configure-maintenance, service-start, service-complete, depreciate, dispose. Configure once per train; explicit immutable service/asset terms; initial service baseline acquisition timestamp and opening odometer (not inferred real service date), can configure historical R25 train after last activity. Config includes time interval, threshold, restoration target strictly above threshold, fixed service cost/duration, repair cost per missing condition bps and base plus per-bps duration. Reuse existing R25 model maintenanceIntervalM for distance due. Due when time or distance at threshold, repair at condition <= configured threshold. Scheduled and repair components additive if both due. Costs and duration derived and locked at start, sufficient declared cash, safe exact time and money, globally unique job ID, one active service per train, exact R06 ServiceJob decoder. No automatic unlocking on clock; complete explicitly at/after locked end, matching ID; restores condition to max(prior,target), never lowers condition, preserves odometer and R25 wear history. Scheduled completion resets distance/time baseline at actual completion; repair-only does not waive scheduled service (may become due during repair). No second charge at completion. Availability query explicit sim time, status UNCONFIGURED/SERVICE/REPAIR_DUE/SERVICE_DUE/AVAILABLE/DISPOSED. Configured trip rejects active service, due service/repair, disposed asset, retroactive activity, zero condition; R25 legacy unconfigured trip replay remains readable, new live R34 dispatch must require AVAILABLE and authoritative train state (cannot treat UNCONFIGURED as eligible). Other trains independent. No service cancellation, facility/crew scheduling, in-flight reservation lock or cash debit: R34/R36 must check authoritative trip state at service start/disposal and atomically commit job/dispatch/cash with R29/R39; this module sees completed trip receipts only. Time comes from trusted scheduler. Do not infer actual location/base or availability of service facility.
E16 exact elapsed simulation seconds from acquisition, immutable declared acquisition basis (not assumed catalog sticker price), residual and useful life, cumulative floor of max(0,cost-residual)*min(elapsed,life)/life. Difference of cumulative floors per nonoverlapping recognition interval; final remainder reconciles. Residual above basis yields effective residual capped at basis and zero depreciation, no appreciation. Zero basis allowed. Noncash expense receipt with cashExpenseMinor zero; no cash debit. Depreciation during active service or mid-trip does not unlock or obstruct legitimate later completed trip. Separate terminal disposal event (no active service), catches up depreciation, records explicit nonnegative resale proceeds and signed gain/loss versus book value, prevents repeated sale/operation/depreciation, keeps history. No market resale price inferred, no actual cash credit or fleet removal; R29/R36 integration required. Asset projection retains book value at disposal for audit, must exclude disposed asset from active equity valuation R30. Exact original rounding/maintenance eligibility and accounting recognition timing subject to DV-036. Revision checks and pure replay preserve input on failure; actual CAS/journal idempotency R29/R34/R39. Full replay 10000 event cap unchanged, no measured scalability.
